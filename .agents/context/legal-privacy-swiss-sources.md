# Swiss Legal And Privacy Sources

## Scope

Use these sources when updating the public website footer, legal notice, privacy wording, contact form notices, or any future privacy policy.

This file is contextual guidance for agents, not legal advice. Before production launch, verify final wording against the real business details and, if needed, a qualified Swiss legal/privacy professional.

## Sources Consulted

- FDPIC / EDÖB, privacy statements on the internet: https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/en/privacy-statements-on-the-internet
  - Practical relevance: Swiss websites that process personal data should give users a clear, comprehensible privacy statement.
  - The notice should explain who is responsible for data processing, what personal data is collected, why it is processed, how long it is kept, what is shared with third parties, available user choices/rights, and the contact address for privacy questions or rights requests.
  - If a website is offered in several languages, the privacy statement should also be available in those languages.

- FDPIC / EDÖB, duty to provide information: https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/en/duty-to-provide-information
  - Practical relevance: under the Swiss FADP/nFADP, data subjects must be informed in advance and in an adequate, concise, transparent, clear, and readily accessible manner when personal data is collected.
  - Website notices, privacy notices, terms, forms, and similar accessible text can be used to satisfy transparency requirements when appropriate.

- Swiss SME Portal / SECO, business site and data protection: https://www.kmu.admin.ch/en/business-site-and-data-protection
  - Practical relevance: Swiss companies that process personal data are subject to the new Federal Act on Data Protection, in force since September 1, 2023.
  - Useful principles for this project: inform users before collecting personal data, keep privacy by design/default in mind, and collect only what is needed for the stated purpose.

## Current Footer Interpretation

- The current contact form does not store emails or contact requests in a database.
- The form collects only the submitted contact/request details and sends them by email through Resend.
- The stated purpose should remain narrow: Ruth uses the submitted email and request details to reply to the client and provide the requested massage service.
- Avoid implying that no technical processing occurs, because Resend processes the submitted email message as the email service provider.
- Keep the legal/privacy copy available in French, English, and Spanish.
- For future medical intake, allergies, medication, or database features, also review `.agents/context/privacy-medical-data.md`.
