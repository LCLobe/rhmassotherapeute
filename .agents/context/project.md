# Project Context

## Overview

RH Massotherapeute is a portfolio and booking website for a therapeutic massage service.

The site should help potential clients quickly understand the service, feel trust in the practitioner, review available treatments, and contact or book an appointment.

The initial services catalog is tracked in `.agents/context/services.md`.

Brand colors and visual style notes are tracked in `.agents/context/brand.md`.

Layout and website inspiration notes are tracked in `.agents/context/design-direction.md`.

Booking and contact decisions are tracked in `.agents/context/booking-contact.md`.

## Audience

- People looking for therapeutic massage services.
- Clients who need clear information before booking.
- Returning clients who want a simple way to schedule future appointments.

## Location And Service Area

- Main location/service area: Yverdon-les-Bains and surroundings.

## Experience Goals

- Calm, professional, and trustworthy.
- Clear path from landing page to services and booking.
- Simple contact flow for users who are not ready to book immediately.
- Flexible enough to support future payments and ecommerce.
- Internationalized experience in French, English, and Spanish.
- Personal and practitioner-led, not corporate or clinic-like.

## Internationalization

- Supported languages: French, English, and Spanish.
- Failsafe fallback language: English.
- Content and navigation should be planned so all public views can be translated.

## Open Product Questions

- Practitioner biography and credentials.
